I had various motivations for building a personal website, but the most
important one was for creating a central hub to store bits and pieces
of myself. To that end, it needed to be an overarching cover for all the
things I wanted to reveal about myself, which is where the word 'canopy'
comes from. In terms of a forest, it is the high layer of leaves and
branches at the top, and though slightly unintuitive if you constantly
work with trees in the data structure sense, I thought it was fitting.

Most of this site is pretty standard; the main point that I am quite
happy with is the side scroll (only viewable on computer), which
emulates a growing vine when you scroll down and retracts when scrolling
back up, much like dangling vines you might see falling from trees in
a larger forest.

Though I haven't gotten to it yet, I think it would be quite interesting
to add a treehouse-style header to match the name as well!
